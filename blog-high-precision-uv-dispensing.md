## 1. The Importance of Dispensing Accuracy

When dealing with UV (Ultraviolet) or shadowless glues, even a micro-fluctuation in the volume or placement of the adhesive can lead to:
*   **Insulation Failure**: Inadequate coverage on PCBs.
*   **Structural Weakness**: Uneven bonding in mechanical assemblies.
*   **Aesthetic Defects**: Visible "runs" or bubbles in decorative applications like Mahjong coloring.

Our flagship 3-axis automated systems are engineered to deliver a **repeat accuracy of ±0.03mm**, ensuring that every drop is placed exactly where it belongs, every single time.
