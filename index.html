Frequently Asked Questions

What is an automatic dispensing machine?

An automatic dispensing machine is precision equipment that dispenses materials like epoxy, polyurethane, or silicone onto products. HongJun is a leading automatic dispensing machine manufacturer in China, offering epoxy resin dispensing systems for PCB, NEV sensors, and electronics. We specialize in vacuum potting machine and encapsulation equipment solutions.

Do you provide glue dispensing robots in China?

Yes, HongJun specializes in glue dispensing robots and automated dispensing solutions. Our systems feature high precision dispensing valves and customizable configurations for various industrial applications including precision potting machines.

What is an epoxy resin dispensing system?

An epoxy resin dispensing system is designed for precise metering and mixing of epoxy materials. HongJun's epoxy resin dispensing systems ensure accurate ratio control, bubble-free dispensing, and consistent quality for electronics potting and encapsulation equipment.

Do you have desktop dispensing machines for PCB?

Yes, we offer desktop dispensing machines for PCB that are compact and ideal for R&D labs and small-scale production. These machines provide precision dispensing with minimal footprint, perfect for vacuum potting applications.

Are you a high precision dispensing valve supplier?

HongJun is a high precision dispensing valve supplier with pneumatic and electric valve options. Our valves feature flow rate control, material compatibility for various viscosities, and reliable performance for precision potting machines.

What is a vacuum potting machine?

A vacuum potting machine is specialized encapsulation equipment that removes air bubbles during the potting process. HongJun's vacuum potting machines ensure bubble-free encapsulation for NEV sensors, automotive electronics, and precision potting applications.
